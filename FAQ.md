# Frequently Asked Questions

## For Contributors

### How do I find bounties to work on?
Browse the issues labeled "Stellar Wave" and "good first issue" for beginner-friendly tasks.

### How do I claim a bounty?
Comment \`/attempt\` on the issue. A maintainer will assign it to you.

### How are bounties paid?
Bounties are paid in USDC on the Stellar network to the wallet address you provide in your PR description.

### Can I work on multiple bounties?
Yes, but complete one before starting another to avoid blocking others.

### What if my PR isn't merged?
Address the reviewer's feedback. If the issue is no longer relevant, a maintainer will unassign you.

## For Maintainers

### How do I approve a bounty submission?
Review the PR, merge it, then comment \`/approve\` on the issue to trigger payment.

### How do I add new bounties?
Create a new issue with the "Stellar Wave" label and describe the task clearly.

### What's the review timeline?
We aim to review submissions within 48 hours.

## General

### What is the Stellar network?
Stellar is a decentralized payment network used for fast, low-cost cross-border transactions and token transfers.
